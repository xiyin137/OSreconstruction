The same real coordinate space is used for Euclidean and relativistic smearing; their respective geometry and axioms are specified below. Interpretation. 𝒮n\mathcal S_n is the usual space of smooth, rapidly decreasing joint test functions for an nn-point distribution. The index kk controls decay at infinity, and ℓ\ell controls derivatives in all real spacetime coordinates. The derivative Dℓf(x)D^\ell f(x) is a multilinear map, so its operator norm controls all derivative directions at once. The supremum norm on configurations fixes the particular numerical seminorm convention used here.

𝒮n\mathcal S_n has pointwise complex vector-space operations. Its topology is specified by the following neighborhood base at each f∈𝒮nf\in\mathcal S_n:

𝒰(f;A,ε)={g∈𝒮n:∀(k,ℓ)∈A, pk,ℓ(n)(g−f)<ε},A⊆ℕ2 finite,ε∈ℝ,ε>0.\mathcal U(f;A,\varepsilon)=\left\{g\in\mathcal S_n: \forall(k,\ell)\in A,\ p_{k,\ell}^{(n)}(g-f)<\varepsilon\right\}, \quad A\subseteq\mathbb N^2\text{ finite},\quad\varepsilon\in\mathbb R,\quad\varepsilon>0.

Interpretation. This is the Schwartz topology: convergence requires convergence in every weighted derivative seminorm pk,ℓ(n)p_{k,\ell}^{(n)}. A continuous complex-linear functional for this topology is a tempered distribution. This specifies the meaning of temperedness when the later axioms are imposed on the full test space.

A distribution family, before imposing any axioms, is

Familyd=∏n∈ℕ(𝒮n⟶ℂ).\operatorname{Family}_d=\prod_{n\in\mathbb N}(\mathcal S_n\longrightarrow\mathbb C).

Interpretation. Familyd\operatorname{Family}_d is the ambient space for an entire hierarchy of smeared correlation functions. At this stage its components are arbitrary maps. Linearity, continuity, normalization, symmetry and positivity become requirements only when stated in the relevant record. In particular, quantification over this ambient space in the uniqueness theorem must be read with exactly the hypotheses displayed there.

All integrals use the finite product of ordinary Lebesgue measures in the displayed real coordinates. Interpretation. The integrals express ordinary spacetime smearing when the integrand is integrable. Their value-zero convention outside the integrable case is part of the formal definition, rather than a prescription for regularizing a divergent correlation function. The mass-one convention at arity zero makes scalar smearing agree with evaluation on the unique empty configuration.
